FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InfluenceX before getting started.

How does InfluenceX work for creators?
Creators can join InfluenceX, build their profile with social stats, and immediately start receiving brand collaboration offers matched to their niche and audience. Payments are secured and released via milestones.
How do brands find creators?
Brands can explore creator profiles, use AI-powered filters by niche, follower count, engagement rate, and platform. Post a campaign and get matched instantly, or reach out directly to creators you love.
Is InfluenceX free to join?
Yes, creators can join for free with no upfront costs. Brands can explore the platform and creators without setup fees. Premium features are available for scaling teams and power users.
How do payments work?
Payments are handled securely through milestone-based escrow releases. Funds are held safely until collaboration tasks are completed and approved, protecting both creators and brands.
Can I use multiple social platforms?
Absolutely. Connect Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, and more to maximize your reach and unlock more brand deal opportunities across all channels.
